Late Fusion Classifier

Conclusion

  • Clean accuracy is not evidence of balanced reasoning: fusion beat audio-only by just +0.58 pp.
  • The fusion head, not the visual encoder, failed: a −39.39 pp fallback gap under missing audio.
  • Known absence is repairable by availability fallback; degraded-but-present input needs calibrated reliability.

TakeawayA robust system must know which modality to trust as evidence changes, and fail gracefully when none is trustworthy.
